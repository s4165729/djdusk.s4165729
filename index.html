The Artist

DJ DUSK

DJ Dusk is an electronic music producer and techno DJ immersed in night club culture. Her sound is raw, relentless, and impossible to ignore.

“The music has to make you feel something you haven’t felt before.”

Born in Shanghai and raised on the streets of London, DJ Dusk found her passion for music — specifically techno — in the underground club scene that never sleeps.

“Memory” is her debut visual release — a piece of art she plays at the back of her performances and concerts.
